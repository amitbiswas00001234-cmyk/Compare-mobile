📦 The Perfect Unboxing Checklist

Just received your phone? Perform these 7 checks to ensure you didn't get a defective unit.

Physical Inspection

Check for scratches, loose buttons, or a wobbly charging port.

Dead Pixel Test

Open a pure white and pure black image. Look for tiny dots that don't change color.

Charging Speed

Plug in the original charger. Ensure "Fast Charging" or "Dash/Dart" is shown on screen.

IMEI Verification

Dial *#06# and match the number with the box. Verify on the brand's official portal.

Camera Focus & OIS

Shake the phone slightly while recording video. It should look smooth, not shaky.

Network & WiFi

Check if 5G signal is stable and WiFi doesn't drop when you move 10 feet away.

Sensor Check

Verify fingerprint, face unlock, and proximity sensor (screen turns off during calls).
